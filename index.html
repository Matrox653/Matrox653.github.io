Behind the timeline

Editing is where a video actually becomes a video — pacing, sound, and motion working together so nothing feels like it's dragging. I edit in DaVinci Resolve, stacking footage, text, and sound design across multiple tracks, then fine-tuning transitions and effects with keyframes and splines until every cut lands on beat.
